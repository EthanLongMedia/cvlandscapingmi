The Michigan Year

One crew, twelve months, nothing left to chase.

Most people call a landscaper twice a year and hope for the best. A property looks its best when the same crew works it on a schedule — here's what that year looks like.

March – April

Spring Cleanup

Winter debris hauled off, beds edged and cleaned out, first cut of the year. The property gets its shape back.

01
April – October

Weekly Mowing

Mow, trim, blow — the same day every week. Stripes laid clean and the walks left spotless.

02
May – September

Fertilization

A monthly step program that feeds the turf and pushes out weeds before they take a foothold.

03
August – September

Core Aeration

Plugs pulled to break up compaction so water, air and seed reach the root zone ahead of fall.

04
October – November

Fall Cleanup

Leaves off, final cut, beds put to bed. Everything set so spring starts from a clean slate.

05
November – March

Snow & Ice

Commercial plowing, salting and walk clearing — through the storm, not the morning after it.
